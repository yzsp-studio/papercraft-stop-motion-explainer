## 🇬🇧 English

For creators who explain science, education or general knowledge through handmade paper craft: give it a topic, a knowledge point or materials, and optionally an audience, duration, aspect ratio and delivery type. After stage confirmations it distils the learning goal and visual metaphor, designs paper puppets, layered sets and props, and produces previews, image and video prompts, storyboards, camera moves, transitions, sound direction, negative prompts and a review checklist — a papercraft stop-motion explainer package ready to build. It can also deliver only still frames, series images, short-video prompts or a storyboard. It covers paper cutting, pop-up books, layered paper sculpture and miniature stop motion, but not ordinary 2D cartoons, line art, live action or non-papercraft science communication.

### ✨ What it does

- Topic or knowledge point → a complete papercraft stop-motion explainer package
- Distils the learning goal and visual metaphor, then designs puppets, layered sets and props
- Outputs storyboard, camera moves, transitions, sound direction and negative prompts
- Confirmation cards gate each stage; you can stop at still frames or series images
- Enforces foreground/midground/background depth and frame-by-frame handmade stop-motion feel
- Long production documents go to the canvas, leaving chat to a summary and next step
